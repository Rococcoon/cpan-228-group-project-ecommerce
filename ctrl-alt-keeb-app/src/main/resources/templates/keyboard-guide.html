Mechanical Keyboard Guide

Learn about common split-keyboard layouts and choose the right keyboard for your typing style.

Compact Layouts

Compact keyboards such as Corne and Sweep reduce the number of keys and encourage the use of programmable layers.

They are suitable for users who value portability and minimal hand movement.

Medium Layouts

Layouts such as Lily58 and Sofle provide additional keys while maintaining an ergonomic split design.

They offer a balance between compact size and access to common keyboard functions.

Larger Layouts

Ergodox and Moonlander keyboards include larger thumb clusters and more physical keys.

They are useful for users who prefer dedicated keys and extensive customization.

Choosing a Keyboard

Consider the number of keys, switch type, layout, portability, programmability and available desk space before selecting a keyboard.
